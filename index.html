        How Kavach keeps your messages private
        
      
          
        
        1. Code checks first, always. Plain code looks for OTP and PIN requests, "enter PIN to receive", lookalike bank links, short links, APK installs, "digital arrest", task jobs and more, in English and Hindi. It works offline, and the AI can raise the risk but never lower it.
          2. 
        
        2. Gemini Nano on your device. On Chrome for desktop, Firebase AI Logic runs the AI check with the on-device model. The message never leaves your computer, works offline and costs nothing.
          3. 
        
        3. Masked cloud fallback. On phones and older PCs, Kavach first replaces OTPs, account, card, Aadhaar, PAN, phone numbers, emails and UPI names with labels, then asks Gemini in the cloud. You can see exactly what was sent.
          4. 
        
        4. Fresh scam patterns. New scam rules and alerts arrive through Firebase Remote Config, without an app update.
          5. 
      
        
      
        Kavach is a helper, not a guarantee. When in doubt, don't click, don't pay, and call the bank or office on its official number.
